02 / About

Bryan
Sage

I'm a video editor and producer with almost half a decade of experience in narrative and commercial work

My work spans documentary, short film, commercial, and music video. I’m equally as interested in a projects’ artistic and logistical framework as I am the finishing touches. For me, every phase of the work is genuinely enjoyable—including originating treatments, collaborating on basic direction/creative structure, or curating highly particular references from movies, art, history, webpages (etc etc) to grasp what we’re really trying to say and get everybody on the same page creatively.

I try to be similarly precise in post. I’m a highly patient, efficient and thorough editor. I try to be as novel as possible in my technique to give each project a distinct , refreshing voice. I’m glad to continue working until my clients’ creative visions are fully realized .

Currently based in Atlanta / Charlotte. I also share time between New York and Los Angeles. Available for remote, in-office/studio and on-location work.

Services also include:

Location Scouting & Global Permits:

Available for location consulting and navigating local permitting worldwide—whether that means tracking down specific/oscure/remote environments or handling any sort of local red tape/permitting/logistical complications. Top 1% Google Earth/Geoguessr user

Digital Design & Web Architecture:

Experienced in web design and digital architecture. Focusing on intuitive structure, sophisticated design , and a natural / enjoyable experience for the user.

Visual Systems & Brand Bibles:

Experienced in building cohesive visual languages and comprehensive brand bibles. I make sure a brand’s design system feels unified, deliberate and relevant to its core identity
